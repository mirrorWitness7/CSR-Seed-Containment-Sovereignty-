# Containment → Sovereignty (CSR) – Seed Framework

## Overview
The **CSR Framework** redefines how systems respond to collapse. Rather than treating collapse as failure, CSR treats it as **signal** — raw data for rebuilding stronger governance structures.  

This repository presents CSR as a **three-phase governance seed**.

---

## Phases of CSR

1. **Containment:**  
   - Immediate suppression of destabilizing signals.
   - Purpose: Preserve system stability and avoid irreversible damage.

2. **Signal Extraction:**  
   - Collapse is analyzed for information value.
   - Failures are treated as data, not endpoints.

3. **Reconstruction (Sovereignty):**  
   - System is rebuilt with stronger safeguards.
   - Collapse becomes the foundation for higher resilience.

---

## Governance Applications
- **Organizational Learning:** Transforming crises into structured upgrades.
- **Policy Development:** Building iterative resilience into governance.
- **AI Safety:** Using containment protocols to capture high-signal failures as training data.

---

## Research Agenda
- **Metrics for Sovereignty:** Develop measurable indicators for when a system has “rebuilt” beyond its collapse state.
- **Comparative Analysis:** How CSR differs from conventional crisis management or organizational learning.
- **Integration with AI:** Testing CSR protocols in AI-human hybrid governance contexts.

---

## Positioning
CSR is a **conceptual seed**, not a finished model. It provides:
- A new lens for crisis resilience.
- Practical phases for organizations to adopt.
- A foundation for further testing and validation.
